How ShareSpace Works

1. Find Space

Enter your location and browse a variety of storage options from trusted hosts in your community. Compare prices, sizes, and amenities.

2. Book It

Once you find the perfect space, send a booking request to the host. You'll only be charged after the host confirms your booking.

3. Store Your Stuff

Coordinate a move-in time with your host. Our secure payment system and support team are here to ensure a smooth experience.
